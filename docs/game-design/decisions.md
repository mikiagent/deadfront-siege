# DEADFRONT decision register

**Created:** 2026-10-25. This distinguishes confirmed *desired future game behavior* from present implementation. Entries are the owner's stated rules, with unresolved follow-up marked **OPEN**. Do not treat illustrative formulas or numbers from historical research as owner confirmation.

## Decisions confirmed in the latest design review

| Key | Confirmed requirement | Crucial limitation / open implementation detail |
|---|---|---|
| HOME-01 | Every player owns one invulnerable private home island by default. Workers, animals, equipment, resources and buildings stored there are safe from enemy raids. | Whether safe *PvE* world hazards exist at home is already answered **no enemy attack**; environmental/cosmetic events still unspecified. |
| HOME-02 | Buying a larger private island costs a **hefty price**; the player **abandons** their previous private island and moves to a **fresh new one with higher-level natural resources**. This is **not** an expansion of the old landmass. | New sizes/tiers, price formula, purchase currency, terrain choices, home claim transfer and relocation UX OPEN. |
| HOME-03 | Dismantled building resources from the abandoned island are saved in a **dedicated Ender Chest-like recovery box** for dismantled materials. | Does this box hold original materials at full value or salvage fraction? Does it have slots/capacity? How does existing stored cargo, worksites, livestock, people and undismantled property migrate? All OPEN. |
| LVL-01 | **PvPvE is locked until a player reaches personal level 60 and unlocks level-60 islands.** Lower-level gameplay does not grant PvPvE access. | Whether a tribe retains the zone unlock when the currently controlled adult changes to a newly low-level successor, and actual deployment restrictions, OPEN. |
| LVL-02 | **Strict item-level equipment gate**. A human cannot equip level-N dropped, bought, looted, gifted, crafted or inherited equipment before that *individual* reaches the required level. No downscaled-equipment option selected. | Type-specific exceptions (e.g. food, boat supplies, crafting ingredients), interactions with job skill requirements and NPC equipping OPEN. |
| LVL-03 | Adult apparent age should relate to earned personal level, while offspring mature via food-dependent timers and education. | Numerical age band mapping, chronology vs visuals, eventual elder/death rules OPEN. |
| ECO-01 | Game identity inspired by **Minecraft Skyblock protected-home economy + Durango regional gathering/crafting + ARK creatures/genetics and survival**. Protected home and legitimate non-PvP progression are central, not raid income. | PvE vs PvPvE resource exclusivity/open-ended economy OPEN. |
| ECO-02 | High-level market goods must not skip personal progression. | Equipment hard gate applies; further material/skill/research gate interaction OPEN. |
| PVP-01 | PvP/PvE attacks apply to **voluntarily exposed** remote territorial outposts, never private home. Distinct PvE and PvPvE zones. | Offline exposed-outpost consequences, target-eligibility formula and world persistence OPEN. |
| PVP-02 | **NO player-declared vulnerability or scheduled raid windows.** | Attacker-side expensive siege permits/cooldowns, server alerts, offline defender AI and no-grief recovery period OPEN. |
| PVP-03 | Local alphas should feel strong relative to their region; larger players must not cheaply punch down against newbies. Raiding must be **hard, expensive, potentially lucrative, and realistically defensible**. | Exact level brackets, region ceilings, force power evaluation, payouts, defender edge, cooldown OPEN. |
| PVP-04 | Winning raids may yield salvage from dismantling exposed outpost structures, captured **human tribe members**, captured **dinosaurs**, unshipped resources and equipped items/accessories. | Captive treatment/ownership/rescue, eligibility for children, building recovery rate, raid completion criteria, loss caps OPEN. |
| PVP-05 | Players should keep exceptional individuals, breeding stock, gear and materials at safe home and risk only disposable/recoverable frontier assets. **Raiding must not become the best way to progress.** | Whether dangerous zone-exclusive resources are necessary for late-stage lineages/home islands OPEN. |
| LOG-01 | A boat **may depart toward private home during an ongoing raid**. The boat, crew and cargo **cannot be attacked/intercepted once underway homeward**. | Protection of *outbound* boat, loading and dock danger, voyage time, offline jobs OPEN. |
| LOG-02 | Amount of goods saved by a departing boat is constrained by the **boat's level and traits plus the harbor's level and traits**. | Weight/slots formula, inventory reservation behavior, load rates and logistics UI OPEN. |
| TRIBE-01 | Tribe consists of persistent individual humans, separate from a permanent dinosaur roster. Switching playable body **does not reset** existing tribe, individual careers, base or gear. A former avatar can become highly trained base worker. | Which skills/blueprints are per-person vs tribe-global OPEN. |
| TRIBE-02 | Human reproduction is **very expensive even late game**. Descendants inherit potential but must earn their own XP and levels; growth is **timed**, consumes food, quality of food at different stages affects expressed stats, and youth may be educated. | Parentage, stages/rates, teacher slots, trait inheritance math, age-level coordination OPEN. |
| TRIBE-03 | Human and dinosaur genetics can be improved through costly selective breeding; typical wild dinosaurs have spiky and rarely superb stats. Tribe members and dinosaurs can die or be captured if exposed. | Genetic caps, mortality, captive rights and old-member retirement/open playability OPEN. |
| TRIBE-04 | If every human is lost, spawn a low-/mediocre-stat baseline player for recovery. | Where and what happens to exposed claims/property, family trees and captive kin OPEN. |
| TRIBE-05 | Humans have recognizable sex/body build differences as fictional default stat tendencies, with **same intelligence baseline, overlap, no class bans**. Women can excel as ranged/gun/crossbow warriors; great nurture/education/medicine support roles genuinely help fighters and future generations. | Exact distributions, skill versus innate contribution, stat visibility OPEN. |
| PARTY-01 | There are **three shared active Expedition companion slots** for humans/dinosaurs, and a human+dinosaur mount pair counts as **one slot**. Riding improves control/coordination and permits rider weapons while reducing some autonomous animal attacks. | Player-avatar mount slot rules, death/dismount, deployment side effects and tactics OPEN. |
| WORK-01 | Workers have human stats, equipment, and role aptitudes. Usually choose work they *can* do and prefer specializations, while a buildable Monitoring Stand directs priorities. | WORK-02 | AI workers stop work-tool use just before breakage (at 1 durability, e.g. 1/250). | Combat weapons and automated repairs/replacements OPEN. |
| MAT-01 | Gathered items almost always have impactful **negative** traits; positive traits are rare; essentially no neutral lots. Rare named-trait charms may affect gather odds. | Exact probabilities, rates, charm additive vs multiplicative, stack grouping, inheritance across crafting OPEN. |

| UX-01 | Sleep removes exhaustion *continuously*, Sit anywhere valid slowly removes exhaustion and restores Energy; status effects have hover/tap explanations with real numbers; inventory has batch Select -> lock/drop/destroy. | Exact rates, interruption/use rules, object stacking/drop behavior and touch UI OPEN. |

## Conflict-resolution notes

1. **Private island**: older `docs/design/world-design.md` depicts a single permanent home, while later notes proposed expansion or multiple safe islands. The newest confirmed rule is **purchase, abandon, move to fresh higher-resource safe island and salvage vault**. Old private island protection still applies until transfer completes; exact transition rules OPEN.
2. **PvPvE entry**: previous design notes only said "maybe late-game PvP"; now **personal level 60 and level-60 island unlock** is an explicit requirement. Do not infer how an old level-60 ancestor's unlock applies to a new low-level successor.
3. **Equipment**: older documents mention hard gate vs effective cap as alternatives. The owner chose **hard gate**. Enforce on effective playable characters; exact item categories still to specify.
4. **Age**: level-related adult age and timed growth are **both required**; neither cancels the other. Needs a clear state machine.
5. **PvP scheduling**: prior workshop listed windows as a recommended option. The owner rejected this. No player-scheduled raid windows.
6. **Raid economy**: previous docs referred to loot primarily as exposed stockpiles; expanded owner-approved candidates include salvage, captives and equipment. Capture rules must be carefully specified to avoid overpowering economic progression.
7. **Research PRD**: original Durango implementation/snapshots contain era-dependent PvP, aging and no breeding. Those are **historical research**, not source requirements for this future Deadfront design.
8. **Code reality vs target design**: current Godot project is solo-first with existing skill/item inventory and home island logic. This ledger does not establish that future switching, genetics, remote raid capture, larger islands or player-market protection already exist.
