# WildLands MMO | Vision workshop

> **Status:** Open product discussion  
> **Vision reference:** [WildLands MMO Game Vision](WildLandsMMO_Game_Vision.md)  
> **Purpose:** Record decisions about the player's experience, the multiplayer shape and the recurring gameplay loop without conflating those decisions with the current implementation.

## What is already established

WildLands MMO is an isometric prehistoric survival and civilization-building RPG with a protected, persistent private home, a personal human character, evolving tribe members, dinosaurs, differentiated materials, processing and physical logistics. Optional endgame PvPvE creates meaningful risk at exposed remote settlements, never at the private home. Player specialization in craftsmanship, trading and animal breeding should be as valid as combat progression.

## Session loop under discussion

The intended backbone is:

**Prepare safely at home → travel for a specific purpose → encounter meaningful wilderness risk → gather, hunt, tame or explore → decide what to carry or abandon → extract cargo and return → craft, trade, train or build → unlock more ambitious goals.**

When tribe systems mature, production can continue through actual workers while the player undertakes expeditions. That does not yet establish how much of the player's time is spent manually managing jobs versus personally adventuring.

### Questions, round 1: the identity of the experience

**V-01: What should hook a new player within the first 20 to 30 minutes?**

Possible directions: (A) danger and escape, fighting to survive a wild island; (B) the satisfying gather → craft → shelter loop and visible home ownership; (C) bonding with a first dinosaur and immediately feeling the difference; (D) a deliberate mixture with a clear order. All can appear eventually, but one should become the memorable first promise.

**V-02: What should a typical 30 to 60 minute session look like after the player has a starter base and their first dinosaur?**

Possibilities: (A) one self-contained expedition, extraction and crafting reward; (B) several shorter expeditions punctuated by workshop and worker management; (C) a goal-driven sandbox with no prescribed pattern. What share should be field adventure compared with base/economy time?

**V-03: What does MMO mean moment to moment?**

Possibilities: (A) primarily private-home plus instanced expedition islands, with visible player trading/social hubs; (B) shared persistent regions and public wilderness encounters; (C) a hybrid, with private home and scalable expedition instances but shared hubs, certain public maps, markets and frontier settlements. How common should meeting a stranger in the world feel?
