# WildLands MMO | Vision workshop

> **Status:** Open product discussion  
> **Vision reference:** [WildLands MMO Game Vision](WildLandsMMO_Game_Vision.md)  
> **Purpose:** Record decisions about the player's experience, the multiplayer shape and the recurring gameplay loop without conflating those decisions with the current implementation.

## What is already established

WildLands MMO is an isometric prehistoric survival and civilization-building RPG with a protected, persistent private home, a personal human character, evolving tribe members, dinosaurs, differentiated materials, processing and physical logistics. Optional endgame PvPvE creates meaningful risk at exposed remote settlements, never at the private home. Player specialization in craftsmanship, trading and animal breeding should be as valid as combat progression.

These choices determine the **structure of player freedom** without locking details the owner has not decided. They complement, rather than override, the existing confirmed private-home safety and PvPvE rules in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Sharpened gameplay loops

The first half hour introduces **three mutually reinforcing promises**: "I can survive this world," "what I make matters," and "these dinosaurs can become companions or threats." The exact tutorial chronology and whether the first animal is actually tamed in that window are not yet determined.

After onboarding, the game does **not** prescribe a session pattern. Players may spend their time in several interconnected loops:

| Player-chosen loop | Experience |
|---|---|
| **Expedition** | Prepare → travel → face risk → gather, hunt, tame or discover → extract → keep, craft or sell |
| **Maker** | Find or buy unusual inputs → process → assemble quality goods → equip, deploy or trade |
| **Breeder** | Discover individuals → care and train → select traits → develop a lineage or specialist role |
| **Settlement** | Plan → gather or buy inputs → build → assign and support real workers → gain new capabilities |
| **Social/economic** | Visit a shared place → meet, cooperate or trade → invest gains in a chosen pursuit |

An expedition is a signature cycle, **not** a required daily or hourly activity. Private-base progress and trade-only sessions are real gameplay. These paths depend on one another economically, even when individual players do not personally participate in all of them.

The hybrid world makes these loops visible across spaces: safe homes for permanent creation, instanced expeditions for focused adventure, shared hubs and public zones for meeting others, and optional PvPvE settlements for competitive stakes.

### Round 1 results and interpretation

- **V-01: D.** A balanced opening including survival, crafting and dinosaur encounters; no one subsystem owns the entire introduction.
- **V-02: C.** Self-directed sandbox sessions. Expeditions are one available loop, not a daily checklist.
- **V-03: C.** Hybrid MMO: private homes, instanced expeditions, shared public locations/hubs and optional frontier settlements.

### Round 2: owner answers confirmed (2026-10-27)

**V-04: C, Open-world first tame.** The first animal is found and tamed through exploration, not granted or obtained through a forced guided introduction. An early dinosaur encounter remains an opening promise even if the tame itself comes later.

**V-07: Meaningful but recoverable setbacks.** Human and dinosaur companions can die or be captured; dropped equipment and cargo can be **recovered**, not guaranteed returned. The game provides many replacement avenues: ordinary tamable dinosaurs, mediocre/basic tribe recruits arriving intermittently by boat at private homes, worker production, and better recruitment odds or volumes at exposed PvPvE outposts. Protect prize loot and choose which resources and individuals to risk. Living captives keep the 72-real-hour game-priced ransom system. Death of a human may eventually allow an extraordinarily costly late-game revival. Recovery durations, death eligibility and rates are not yet confirmed.

**V-09: A, Strongly solo-friendly.** Survival, breeding, work, settlements and peaceful progress are complete activities for a solo player. Late-game multiplayer, group raids, cooperative play, outposts, PvP and PvPvE create meaningful shared opportunities rather than an obligatory beginner group.

**V-15: Autonomous gathering.** Physically present tribe workers operate mining/logging sites automatically, with distinct speeds and quality outcomes. A level-50 site can produce items in the level-45 to level-50 range. The *exact distribution*, how skills alter it and production-rate formula remain OPEN.

**V-16: Exceptional late-game revival.** A dead player-controlled human or human tribe member can be restored at extraordinary cost, used rarely. The method, remains requirement, life/skill restoration, rarity ceiling and numeric formula remain OPEN. **Not a decision to revive dinosaurs.**

### Round 3: owner answers confirmed (2026-10-27)

**M-01: C, Two revival paths.** The normal path retrieves the individual's physical remains and is very costly. Missing or inaccessible remains do **not** categorically prevent revival: a second path is possible but **extraordinarily more expensive** and must preserve unique identity. The resurrection method, evidence required for the fallback, retained level/skills/EVs, eligibility window, repeat limits and numerical cost are still OPEN.

**M-02: C, Scarcity plus costly purification.** Most raw lots carry drawbacks and exceptional positive traits are rare. Skilled processors can **reduce or remove eligible negative properties**, but only at substantial material/labor/station expense. Purification is deliberate and cannot freely create positive traits or duplicate valuable materials. Specific negative odds (96% candidate), positive odds (7% candidate), treatment permissions and costs are still OPEN.

**M-03: C, Three dimensions of gathering expertise.** Better miners and loggers work faster, shift item-level quality toward the **site's existing upper bound** and have a **modestly increased chance of rare positive source traits**. A level-50 site still yields only level-45 through level-50 resources. Exact productivity, quality and trait-odds curves remain OPEN.

**M-04: Combat pace.** No owner answer yet. Remains OPEN in the [Open Questions register](WildLandsMMO_Open_Questions.md).

## Decision entries

**2026-10-27, owner-confirmed vision answers:**

- **V-01: D, A combination.** Onboarding should expose survival, crafting and dinosaurs together. Neither a fixed scripted sequence nor the timing of a successful first tame was selected.
- **V-02: C, Open sandbox.** Typical play is goal-driven without a mandated 30 to 60 minute itinerary. Peaceful industry, breeding, exploration, trade and other chosen goals remain legitimate.
- **V-03: C, Hybrid world.** Private homes, instanced expeditions, shared public zones, social/trading hubs and optional frontier settlements are intended to coexist.

**Implementation status:** These are confirmed *desired game experience* decisions, not proof that multiplayer infrastructure, tutorials or every sandbox profession is currently implemented. The working-title rebrand affects vision documents only; it does not rename the current Godot project, game assets, repository or deployment.

**2026-10-27, Round 2 and systems owner answers:** V-04=C; V-07=meaningful loss/recoverable drops with basic recruitment and animal supply; V-09=A; V-15=automated mining/logging, bounded item-level site yields; V-16=rare, extremely costly late-game dead-human revival.

**2026-10-27, Round 3 answers:** M-01=C (remains normally, vastly costlier fallback without remains); M-02=C (rare positives/common drawbacks, costly skill-based mitigation/removal); M-03=C (worker speed, bounded level quality and modest positive trait odds). No exact coefficients were approved.

**Unresolved:** M-04 combat feel and the detailed numerical and eligibility questions from M-01 to M-03. Track these in [WildLands MMO Open Questions](WildLandsMMO_Open_Questions.md) rather than repeating them as forced choices.
