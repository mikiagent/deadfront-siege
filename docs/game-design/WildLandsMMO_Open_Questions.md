## How to maintain this document

1. If the owner says **"I don't know yet"**, write the question here with context, meaningful alternatives and the gameplay consequence. Keep it **OPEN**.
2. Do not interpret a selected *design direction* as approving an illustrative percentage, timer, skill ratio or formula.
3. When the owner answers, record the exact decision and date in [decisions.md](decisions.md), update its home proposal/wikilinks, and mark/remove the corresponding **OPEN** entry so the question is not asked repeatedly.
4. If two reference documents disagree, flag the conflict here; do not silently choose a winner.
5. Ask a small number of questions per workshop round, and **offer to park difficult ones here** rather than forcing an arbitrary answer.
