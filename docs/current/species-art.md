# Species art — the owner's creature reference sheets

Milan's reference sheets, one section per species, saved as JPEG copies (about 1600 px wide) under
`docs/art/species/<species>/`. The 2 MB PNG originals stay in the `species-ref`, `rex-ref/owner` and
`ankylo-ref` worktrees. Each image is labelled with its role:

- **Regular**: the normal look of the species.
- **Alpha skin**: the Alpha variant, which is the angrier sheet.
- **Sex variant**: male or female look of the same species.
- **Reference only**: kept for detail or proportions, not a skin to build.

**Status** means: *in game with a model* = `game/assets/creatures/<id>/` has a GLB on
`claude/mmo-overhaul` (2026-10-30); *no model yet* = no GLB yet; *new species* = not in the
current roster. Ankylosaurus is the only species here whose sheets were made by Codex rather than the owner.

**Removed:** Protoceratops was removed by the owner on 2026-10-31 ("the animations suck and the model is
unappealing"). It had no sheet here; its model is deleted and tamed ones load as zebraceratops.
