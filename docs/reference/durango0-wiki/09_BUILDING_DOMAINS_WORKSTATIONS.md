# Construction, buildings, workstation queues and domains

## Two different flows
1. Craft portable components and furniture through recipes.
2. Place a building site in a legal area, supply exact construction materials, perform building work, and activate the completed structure.

Construction category level determines blueprint eligibility while Building/Furniture Ability affects demanding tasks. Building level and material quality are separate. Station type and minimum station level gate recipes.

## Important functional quality
- Lightweight on appropriate storage structures can increase spaces.
- Beautiful Pattern can increase Comfort and improve resting conditions.
- Strong Disinfectant can grant Antibiotic effects in suitable furniture/buildings.
- High Density can enhance structure durability.
- Special properties can reduce delegated production duration or increase queue capacity.

The restoration estimates Comfort as Fatigue −0.075/s per level and Antibiotic as Health +0.0075/s per level. **These coefficients should not be presented as original Nexon confirmed.**

## House modules
Modular floors, walls, roofs, doors and furniture build settlements. Material species can influence wall/roof pattern; the current restoration's mixed-material pattern selection is explicitly modified/reconstructed.

## Claim system
Separate private Tamed Island domains, renewable shared-island claims, clan territories and strategic outposts. Land rights, building ownership and item/container ownership must be separate. Claim-cell size, plot caps, renewal rules and capacity progression are version-sensitive.

## Workstations
A workbench, kiln, drying rack, loom, kitchen and pen each support different recipe families. Each station maintains type, completed state, effective level, permissions, job slots, running jobs, input/output escrow and timestamps. Never allow an unfinished building to run recipes.
