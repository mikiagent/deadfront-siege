# UI pass, 2026-10-17

Mockups for these screens are on a Claude design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/YZfV9qu2EDxAxqN24RYsXz
The canvas is private to its owner until shared. It holds five boards: the menu, character and bag, animals and growth, the in-world taming plate, and the bag on a phone.

## What changed in the game

| Screen | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Menu | Bag, Craft, Map, Save, Debug. No full screen, no controls, no hint that Esc or Start opens it. | Adds Animals, Skills, Full screen and Controls. Shows key hints on each row and says "Esc or Start opens this menu". A Controls page lists keyboard and gamepad. New survivors get a one-time tip pointing at it. |
| Bag | 150 px cells with a tiny icon and a count, no item names. Stats as a plain text list. Equipment cut off at the right edge. | Cells capped at 104 px, with the item name and count. Stats are aligned rows with an XP bar and a bag fullness bar. Columns are centred with margins, the item card is a panel, and the buttons are styled. Phone layout fits. |
| Animals | Three wide empty "ACTIVE" bars, plain text stats, grey background, a big empty area. | Header with a count, three "with you" cards, a roster with a portrait badge, grade chip and status, and a detail panel with a portrait, XP and hunger bars, stat bars and a growth table. Phone stacks the roster over the detail. |
| Taming | A small world label and a bare health bar. | A teal capture line at 30% on the target's health bar, and a "Taming" panel under the vitals with three steps: wear it down, knock it over, feed it. The current step is highlighted and feeding shows progress. |

## How it was checked

- Unit tests, smoke test and the UI screenshot lab pass.
- Screenshots reviewed at 1600 by 900 and 390 by 844 for the menu, controls page, bag and animals screen.
- The taming plate was reviewed in a capture-lab screenshot at 1600 by 900 only.
