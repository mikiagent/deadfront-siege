# Durango: Wild Lands | Crafting and Materials Research

**Research date:** 2026-10-27

**Scope:** historical recipe transcription and original-era mechanics to inform an independent game design. This file is research, not Deadfront Siege canon.

## Evidence and coverage

- **90** recipes with ingredient quantities transcribed from the community mastersheet.
- **88** additional recipes with names confirmed by a fan restoration's original skill-tree data, but ingredient quantities not established.
- **186** material names and research-classification entries.
- **25** material processing and transformation operations.
- The fan restoration reports 720 original recipe records (625 crafting, 73 processing, 22 modification). **This curated file is not an export of all 720.**
- Square-bracketed `[title inferred]` records have an ingredient list in the historical community sheet but an evidently incorrect title in that source.
- Fan restoration code can diverge from the released MMO. Check its source grades before treating formulas as historical fact.

## Important crafting mechanics and design lessons

1. **Crafted intermediates have value.** Axe heads, grips, bow limbs, bowstrings, ropes, cut pillars, processed planks and metal accessories have their own production roles.
2. **Processing changes a material rather than always replacing it.** Trimming, tanning, drying, waterproofing and metal treatment can add tags or properties to the underlying material.
3. **Material inheritance matters.** In the historical community guide, the main ingredient is the main attribute source. Original client and current fan restoration may vary in exact formulas.
4. **Workstation gates create progression.** Construction recipes enable kilns, workbenches, looms and drying racks, which enable more advanced materials and weapons.
5. **Resource geography creates trade.** Specific minerals, plant types and animal-part traits are tied to locations, levels or species.
6. **Construction blueprints are a related, separate data family.** Do not assume the 720 item/processing/modification recipe count includes every buildable structure.
