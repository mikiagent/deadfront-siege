# 18. Domain banner and claimed land (owner, 2026-10-20)

Owner's words, verbatim: "When entering player's claimed regions, it says "Player's Domain" with their profile picture."

References: `docs/reference/durango-domain-claims.webp` (a claimed beach camp) and `docs/reference/durango-house-4x4.webp` (a claimed farm with a house). The rest of this file is the orchestrator's reading.

- **Domain banner.** On entering another player's (or your own) claimed region, the HUD shows "<name>'s Domain" with that player's portrait. The map shows the same label (spec 15).
- **Claim edges.** Claimed land is outlined on the ground by a dotted yellow line, visible in both shots.
- **Inside a domain.** Buildings and props carry small white-icon name plates: Makeshift Fence, Makeshift Tent, Moss Hut, Makeshift Workbench, Makeshift Pen (holding a tamed zebraceratops), Cooking Bonfire, Road Sign, Fish Trap, Oven, Square Wooden Chair, Basic Workbench, House, Small Field.
