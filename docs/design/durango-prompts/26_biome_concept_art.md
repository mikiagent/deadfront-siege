## What each concept shows (use as biome targets, including the starter island)

**Canyon oasis / savanna.**
- Red sandstone cliffs and mesas with waterfalls dropping into a palm oasis.
- A braided turquoise river with reeds, white grass plumes and lily pads.
- Golden savanna with acacias, termite mounds and red-flowered aloes.
- Pale blue crystal clusters in the rocks (ore nodes).
- A camp of thatched huts and hide tents, a fire, fences and a log bridge.

**Volcanic wetland.**
- Black basalt cliffs and spires, smoking volcanoes with lava, and steam vents.
- Yellow sulphur mounds and black obsidian crystals (ore nodes).
- Turquoise hot springs with cream travertine terraces, and red soil patches.
- Araucaria (monkey-puzzle) trees, tree ferns, palms and berry bushes.
- Sauropods wading.

**Savanna river delta.**
- A golden grassland cut by a winding blue river with reed beds, lily pads and fallen logs.
- Palms, acacias, termite mounds, and granite boulder piles with crystals.
- A camp with thatched huts, a watchtower, drying racks and a jetty.

## What this means for the islands plan (docs/design/islands-and-loop-plan.md)

**Biome zones read through the ground and the landforms, not only through the trees:**
- red earth and sandstone cliffs;
- black basalt;
- golden grass;
- wet green riverbanks.

**Big landforms carry the look.** These match the owner's "peninsulas and large cliff resources (or non resource props)":
- cliffs, mesas and spires;
- waterfalls;
- rivers with reed beds;
- hot-spring terraces.

**Ore nodes as crystal clusters in rock.** These read at a glance: blue crystals, black obsidian and yellow sulphur.

**Props that sell a biome:**
- termite mounds;
- fallen logs;
- lily pads;
- reed and plume clumps;
- steam vents.

These suit the 2D-card treatment, except buildings, which stay 3D.

## Colours (COLORS job)
- Normal rolls stay inside each species' natural range.
- Rarely, about 1 in 50 (ASSUMPTION), an animal rolls outside that range as a distinct but believable morph: dark, pale, or a
  stronger hue.
- Taming and saving keep the morph.
- The raid shot shows that bold banded patterns are in character for big animals.
